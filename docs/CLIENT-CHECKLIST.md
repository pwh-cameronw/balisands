# Bali Sands — what we need from the client

## Photography
- Landscape supplies hero: sand/mulch bays or a tipper load at the yard (landscape, high-res)
- One photo per supplies category: sands, soils, stones, mulch, limestone/pavers/bagged, edging/tools/retic
- One strong photo per Bali imports category, especially water features and statues (the current site has few that are usable)
- Original high-res files for any photos on the Wix site they want to keep
- Logo in vector format (SVG/AI/EPS) or a large transparent PNG

## Products & pricing
- Full product list per category (names, variants, sizes)
- Pricing: per m³ / per bag / per item, and whether prices are shown publicly or given on request
- Trade pricing: how it works, who qualifies, and whether it's shown online
- How often stock changes (sets whether imports are listed item by item or by category)

## Delivery
- Metro delivery zones or suburbs covered, plus the fee structure (flat, by zone, by distance)
- Minimum and maximum load sizes, and truck types (tipper, bagged, crane)
- Delivery days/times and lead time
- Whether imports are delivered too, or supplies only
- Who handles quote requests (email address/phone number the form should go to)

## Business details
- Confirm hours (Mon–Sat 6–4:30, Sun/public holidays 8–2), address, phone and ABN
- Email address for enquiries
- Social media links (Instagram, Facebook)
- Established year / short story for the About page
- Terms, delivery & returns policy, privacy policy (existing text or approval to draft)

## Website & technical
- Domain registrar login, or DNS access
- Hosting: existing WordPress host, or do we arrange it
- Elementor Pro licence (needed for the quote form), or approval to buy one
- Google Maps API key or Google account access
- Google Business Profile / Analytics access, if they have them
- Wix account access, to export content and set up redirects before shutdown

## Decisions
- Sign-off on homepage design 2a
- Catalogue only (current brief), or plans to sell online later
- Which pages they want at launch (Delivery, About, Trade, Contact, category pages)
